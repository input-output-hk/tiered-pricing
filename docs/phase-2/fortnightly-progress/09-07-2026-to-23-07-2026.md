# [Experimentation](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/54)

This fortnight found and corrected a cross-lane fee inversion, and reran the canonical headline experiments on separated random streams.

**Cross-lane fee inversion.** The standard quote can temporarily rise above the urgent quote. Before the fix, a wallet set the max fee from the urgent quote alone. Such a transaction sometimes failed its endorser-block fee check during an inversion. The fix: wallet choice and every max-fee validity check for an urgent transaction now use `max(standard quote, urgent quote)`. Temporary quote crossings stay permitted and do not alter either controller. Smoke experiments bounded the effect of the fix on the historical evidence. A targeted ten-seed denominator-8 launch-day run found no statistically detectable change in its headline welfare metrics. The matched denominator-16 check and the integrated canonical check were exactly unchanged across all 550 reported scalar values.

**Separated random streams.** The simulator previously drew fresh demand, ranking-block production, and retry jitter from one shared random stream. These are now three independent streams. We then reran the recommended configuration (`both-dynamic-strict-threshold`, target utilisation 0.5, max-change denominator 16, age escape K = 10) against flat fee over the five headline loads (paired seeds 0-9, 2,000 slots each). The results:

- Severe congestion: urgent retained value improves from 43.56% to 50.85% (+7.29 ± 1.29 percentage points, ten of ten seeds). Urgent mean latency falls from 2.98 to 2.50 blocks.
- Mid load: +3.69 ± 1.06 percentage points (ten of ten seeds).
- EB-capacity stress: +8.57 ± 2.24 percentage points (ten of ten seeds).
- Low load: no statistically detectable difference (+0.47 ± 1.62 percentage points, confidence interval spans zero).
- Launch day: +8.15 ± 2.04 percentage points of overall offered value (ten of ten seeds).

The recommendation is unchanged. The [preserved headline record](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/blob/main/docs/phase-2/experiment-results/canonical-headlines.json) holds every table-driving per-seed scalar plus provenance hashes.

Simulator work in support: the `canonical-headlines` sweep manifest and runner, smoke sweeps and comparison scripts for the inversion fix and the canonical construction, and new unit tests for the fee-cap rule.

# [Prototype](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/55)

The prototype reached its completion point this fortnight. Every sub-ticket closed: the ledger rules ([#66](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/66), [#70](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/70), [#71](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/71), [#72](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/72)), the two-lane mempool ([#68](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/68)), and the end-to-end node run ([#73](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/73)) on 10 July, then testing ([#69](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/69)) on 13 July. Complete means all of the following:

- The node builds against the prototype ledger and the two-lane mempool.
- The node runs on a three-node Dijkstra devnet under actor-driven two-lane load, and forges both lanes with live published prices.
- The rule-level test suites pass, and an automated end-to-end smoke run exists.

The parent ticket ([#55](https://github.com/input-output-hk/tiered-pricing/issues/55)) stays open as the index of the work and its devnet observations.

Work continued past that point, to align the prototype with the CIP's recommended construction:

- The controller moved from D = 4 to the D = 8 anchor (13 July), then to the recommended D = 16 with the CIP's signal windows (20-21 July).
- Fee settlement moved to the rb-only premium scope: settle by delivery, not declaration (21 July).
- The forge gained the K = 10 endorser-block announcement age escape, and admission checks one worst-case controller step ahead (20 July).
- The lane feeder bids the max-of-two fee cap (21 July).
- The mempool gained endorser-block lifecycle handling: announced blocks are stripped, a superseded block's transactions come back, and an express reserve stays out of the merge (21-22 July).
- The ledger branch went CI-green, and the demo gained fee-split-pot measurement (13-22 July).
